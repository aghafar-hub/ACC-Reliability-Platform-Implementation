\# Engineering Decisions



\## Equipment\_ID

Equipment\_ID is the master equipment reference across all modules.



\## LP\_ID

LP\_ID identifies the exact lubrication point.



\## Oil Analysis

Oil analysis reports may start from Equipment\_ID.



\## LP Mapping

Final oil analysis history should link to LP\_ID when applicable.



\## Sampling Points

Not all LPs are sampling points.



\## PDF/OCR

PDF/OCR import must create a review screen first.

Never auto-save OCR output as final without engineer confirmation.
